# Business Context

Cloud kitchens operate on tight margins. After aggregator commissions (typically 20–30% of order value), packaging costs, marketing spend, food costs, rent, labor, and utilities, contribution margins on a typical order are often only 5–15%. Even small operational inefficiencies have a meaningful impact on profitability.

## Why This Problem Matters Financially

### Stockouts

Stockouts cause lost sales. Every minute an ingredient is unavailable but its dependent menu items remain listed leads to order acceptances the kitchen cannot fulfill, hurting both revenue and platform ranking.

### Food waste

Food waste hurts margin. In poorly managed kitchens, 8–15% of food cost is thrown away each month due to expiry. A recipe-linked inventory system can cut this significantly.

### Customer experience

Customer experience drives ranking. Rejected orders, missing items, and partial deliveries hurt aggregator ratings, reducing visibility and orders — a downward spiral.
